Generate Audio Files for Driver Safety System

Use this tool to download placeholder audio files for testing:

Option 1: Download Placeholder Beeps

Option 2: Download Real Audio Files

Get free audio from these sources:

  • FreeSound.org - Search for "alarm", "happy music", "calm music"
  • ZapSplat.com - Free sound effects
  • Pixabay Music - Royalty-free music

After downloading: Rename files to:

  • alarm.mp3
  • happy.mp3
  • calm.mp3
  • upbeat.mp3

Place them in: frontend/public/assets/
